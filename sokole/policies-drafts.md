## Refund and return policy

**Right of withdrawal (14 days)**
If you are a consumer in the EU, you may withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of receiving your
order, without giving a reason. To do so, email us at [email] with your order number before the
14-day period ends.

**Condition of returned items**
For reasons of hygiene and health protection, fragrances and body mists can only be returned if they
are **unopened and still in their original sealed packaging** (cellophane intact). Opened or used
products cannot be returned unless they are faulty.
